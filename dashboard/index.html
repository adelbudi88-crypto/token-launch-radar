How to read this board (for the judge)

Rank is by expected value = token odds × implied value (TVL × a multiple fitted from 108 live-token protocols — the size curve alone: forward selection kept size and tested growth, age, chains and the category factor, none of which lowers the held-out miss in both leave-one-out and walk-forward 70/30). Token odds are the share of same-category, same-age peers that already record a token. 12-mo odds are the fitted chance a protocol of this age lists a token within the next 12 months — they fall with age, and they are shown, not used to price expected value. Ranges are 10–90% intervals from 200 bootstrap refits (value) and the rank across the same refits. The verdict nets the farm plan (farmer share − gas over the whole hold − time at the plan's own hours) to FARM / THIN / SKIP, and net per hour separates two FARM rows. The self-check strip above re-derives every row from the fitted coefficients — the code proves every claim on screen.

A note on the record. A protocol on this board records no token in DefiLlama's data — that is not proof it has none. Implied and expected values are this radar's own claim, never make.fun's, and never a prediction that a token will be issued or when: token odds are a discount from the peers' own record, the share of comparable protocols that already record a token. Farm steps are a plan, not financial advice; verify every link on the protocol's own site before you sign anything.
