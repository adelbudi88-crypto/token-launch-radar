How a launch is priced

derived from the snapshot, not a list of names

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × chains

A launch is worth what the market already pays for the same kind of protocol: the peer multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median when fewer than 3 peers price it, clamped to 0.05–1.50. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −80…+200%, weighted 0.7. Maturity is 0.70–1.00 over 4 years tracked with no token (unknown age counts as 365 days). Chains adds 3% per extra chain, up to +12%. Every row above shows its own multiple and multipliers; the detail view shows the arithmetic.
